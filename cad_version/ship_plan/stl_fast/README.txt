PRINT INSTRUCTIONS - FAST HULL (for the print operator)
=======================================================
Basic PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle. No supports needed for anything.

PRINT THESE 2 FILES (+ 3_plate_deck_sled_arm.stl from the stl_batch2 folder)

1. 1_main_hull.stl
   - Print it exactly as placed: flat bottom on the bed, open side UP. Do not flip it.
   - Add a 4 mm brim (big flat base, corners can lift).
   - 0.28 mm layers, 3 walls, 5 bottom layers, 15 % infill.
   - Flow 102-105 % if possible: it has to hold water.

2. 2_plate_beams_and_floats.stl   (2 floats + 2 beams on one plate)
   - Do NOT rotate or move the parts: the beams are 304 mm long and only fit lying diagonally.
   - 0.28 mm layers, 3 walls, 8 % infill.
DO NOT PRINT
   - float_x2.stl and beam_x2.stl: these are COPIES of the parts on plate 2. Print the plate OR these, never both.
   - assembly_floats_at_125.stl  (only for the flow simulation)
   - the preview_*.png pictures are just pictures

Assembly guide for the team: ../guide.md
